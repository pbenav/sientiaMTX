# ⚡ Activities & Workflow — SientiaMTX (v1.2.0)

In earlier versions of SientiaMTX, all work was modeled exclusively around "Tasks". As of **v1.2.0**, SientiaMTX has evolved into a **universal Activities (Items) architecture**.

An activity is not merely a to-do item: it is a polymorphic collaborative work object that faithfully mirrors how modern organizations operate (living documentation, formal decision-making, integrated meetings, multi-channel reminders, shared links, and operational tasks).

---

## 🧩 1. The 7 Activity Types

Each activity in SientiaMTX belongs to a specific subtype with its own dedicated UI, lifecycle, metadata schema, and tooling:

```
                  ┌── Task (task)
                  ├── Document (document)
                  ├── Note (note)
Activity (Item) ──┼── Link (link)
                  ├── Agreement / Decision (agreement)
                  ├── Meeting (meeting)
                  └── Reminder (reminder)
```

---

### 1.1. Task (`task`)
The core operational engine supercharged by the Eisenhower Matrix.
- **Lifecycle**: `Pending` ➔ `In Progress` ➔ `Completed` (or `Blocked`).
- **Prioritization**: Mandatory assignment of Urgency and Importance to populate the Eisenhower Matrix.
- **Subtasks**: Hierarchical nesting with dedicated subtask threads.
- **Timestamps**: Start date, deadline (`due_date`), and automated recording of the exact completion timestamp (`completed_at`).
- **Time Tracking**: Native integration with the real-time stopwatch and effort accounting system.

---

### 1.2. Document (`document`)
Collaborative document management with full in-browser office suite and graphics capabilities:
- **OnlyOffice Suite Integration**: Live simultaneous multi-user editing for `.docx`, `.xlsx`, and `.pptx` documents, hosted securely on your private infrastructure without third-party cloud leakage.
- **Filerobot Image Editor**: Advanced in-app image editor with cropping, rotation, color filters, annotations, and watermarks. Features dedicated `z-index` layer management to prevent modal collisions.
- **Chapters & Structuring**: Organize documents into structured text chapters with individual authoring and timestamps, or directly inject content generated by **Ax.ia**.
- **Version Control & Statuses**: `draft`, `review`, `approved`, `rejected`, and `archived`.

#### 🗂️ Document Library & Hierarchical File Browser
Starting in v1.3.0, the team library (`/teams/{team}/library`) introduces an enterprise-grade hierarchical file browser:

* **Folder & Subfolder Hierarchy**:
  * Multi-level nested folder structure with unlimited depth.
  * **8 Distinct Color Themes** (violet, blue, emerald, amber, rose, indigo, cyan, and gray) to visually differentiate departmental folders and key projects.
  * Switch between **Grid View** (visual cards with contents counters) and **List/Table View** (columns for file size, author, last modified date, and contextual actions) in real time.
* **Fluid Hierarchical Navigation**:
  * **Interactive Breadcrumbs**: Top navigation trail displaying the full path with folder badges and privacy padlocks, enabling one-click jumps to any parent level or back to root.
  * **`.. (Go up one level)` Direct Folder**: Seamless directory backtracking with clickable button and drag-and-drop target.
* **Smart Drag & Drop**:
  * Move any document or folder by simply dragging and dropping it into any target folder card or into the `.. (Go up one level)` button.
  * **Multi-Item Dragging**: If multiple items are selected via checkboxes, dragging any one item transports **the entire selected batch**, displaying a floating badge with the total count of moving assets.
* **Multi-Selection & Floating Bulk Action Bar**:
  * Individual and shift-based checkboxes on all cards and table rows.
  * **Floating Bulk Action Bar**: Emerge at the bottom when one or more items are selected, providing direct actions:
    * **Bulk Move**: Launches a folder tree modal to relocate all selected assets in a single safe database transaction.
    * **Bulk Delete**: Cascade or preserve contents deletion with security confirmation.
  * **Adaptive Context Menus**: Contextual dropdown menus on cards and rows dynamically recognize when an item belongs to a multi-selection, executing the requested command across all selected assets.
* **Security, Visibility & Privacy Inheritance**:
  * **Public Folders**: Visible and accessible to all team members.
  * **Private Folders (🔒)**: Confidential directories restricted exclusively to the folder creator and team managers/coordinators. Marked with clear security padlock badges.
  * **Automatic Privacy Inheritance**: Any document or subfolder created within or moved into a private folder automatically inherits `private` visibility, preventing inadvertent disclosure of confidential assets.
  * **Zero Metadata Leakage**: Document and subfolder counters strictly filter by current user authorization, preventing unauthorized members from inferring the existence or volume of private records.

---

### 1.3. Note (`note`)
Central knowledge repository, quick briefings, and spontaneous ideas:
- **Rich Formatting**: Native support for clean Markdown and sanitized HTML.
- **Pinned Notes (`pinned`)**: Pin crucial reference information to the top of boards or dossiers.
- **AI Voice Memos**: Record audio directly from your microphone with instantaneous speech-to-text transcription powered by Ax.ia.

---

### 1.4. Link (`link`)
Intelligent bookmark and web reference library:
- **Automatic Open Graph Extraction**: When saving external URLs, SientiaMTX asynchronously fetches page metadata to extract title (`og:title`), description (`og:description`), and preview banner (`og:image`), rendering visually informative cards.
- **Broken Link Auditing**: Track resource health statuses (`active`, `broken`, `archived`) to keep team assets current.

---

### 1.5. Agreement & Decision (`agreement`)
Formal record of team consensus, board decisions, and binding guidelines:
- **Structured Fields**:
  - **Rationale (`rationale`)**: Contextual explanation of the business or technical reasoning behind the decision.
  - **Alternatives Considered**: Record of evaluated alternatives and trade-offs.
  - **Impact Level**: Impact classification (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`).
- **Decision Workflow**: States `Proposed` ➔ `Approved` ➔ `Rejected` / `Deferred` / `Superseded`.
- **Internal Member Digital Signature**: Tracking formal approval and digital consent from each assigned decision-maker.

---

### 1.6. Meeting (`meeting`)
Organization, scheduling, and tracking of in-person and digital sessions:
- **Flexible Modalities**:
  - **In-Person**: Physical address, room, or headquarters.
  - **Sientia Meet (Integrated Jitsi) 🎥**: Instant, secure video conference rooms with zero time limits and no external account sign-ups required.
  - **Smart Google Meet 🌐**: Direct sync with Google Calendar and smart link sanitation that auto-expands full URLs, shortlinks, or raw 10-character room codes (`abc-defg-hij`).
- **Agendas & Minutes**: Track planned topics (`agenda`), confirmed attendees, and recorded minutes (`minutes`).
- **Automated Closing**: Scheduled execution via `php artisan app:autocomplete-meetings` closes past meetings once scheduled time and duration expire with a grace period.

---

### 1.7. Reminder (`reminder`)
Time-sensitive notifications and call-to-actions across multiple channels:
- **Multi-Channel Dispatch**: Dispatch notifications via Email, Telegram Bot, WhatsApp, or Web Push notifications.
- **Smart Scheduling**: Configure alerts to fire $N$ minutes before due time (`notify_before_minutes`) or at an exact designated hour (`notify_at_hour`).
- **Live Countdown & Interactive Toggle Switch**: Renders a live countdown timer with an interactive toggle switch to mark reminders completed on demand, stopping the timer and canceling pending alert dispatches.
- **Background CLI Engine**: Automated batch dispatch handled via `php artisan reminders:trigger`.

## ⏱️ 3. Effort Accounting & Time Tracking

To guarantee trustworthy workload tracking and comply with workplace labor regulations, SientiaMTX includes a full-featured **Time Tracking** suite:

```
[Start Timer] ────▶ [Global Sticky Header Banner] ────▶ [Stop Timer]
      │                                                      │
      ▼                                                      ▼
 Record TimeLog (task/activity)                         Calculate minutes & seconds
      │                                                      │
      └─────────────────▶ Effort Accounting Widget ◀─────────┘
```

### Core Tracking Features:
1. **Real-Time Stopwatch**: High-precision display (hours, minutes, seconds) directly inside activity headers and in the global top bar.
2. **Global Active Task Banner**: When a timer is running, a persistent top banner displays current progress, allowing users to pause, restart, or switch tasks seamlessly without losing context.
3. **Effort Accounting Dashboard Widget**:
   - Displays dedicated time breakdowns per user and activity.
   - **Deep Privacy Guaranteed**: If an activity is private, only authorized users can view its title and logged hours.
   - Automatically sorted by the user's most recent time log.
4. **Anomalous Workday Detection (`timelogs:mark-anomalous`)**:
   - Detects accidental forgotten stopwatches running beyond standard shifts or exceeding the **10-hour hard cap**.
   - Computes normalized `effectiveMinutes` to avoid skewing analytics and alerts coordinators for review.

## 🔐 5. Security, Visibility & Permissions Model

To safeguard sensitive institutional data and maintain organizational structure, SientiaMTX implements a granular authorization policy in [`ActivityPolicy`](app/Policies/ActivityPolicy.php).

### 5.1. Fundamental Principle: Read Access ≠ Write Access
* **Making an activity `Public` DOES NOT grant editing permissions to everyone**.
* Public visibility (`visibility = 'public'`) provides **read-only visibility** to all team members.
* Members with read-only access can review content, inspect revision history, and participate in internal notes or attachments, but **cannot change the status or modify core properties** unless explicitly assigned or holding a managerial role.

---

### 5.2. Who can VIEW an activity? (`view`)
A team member can view an activity if:
1. **They are the creator / author** of the activity.
2. **They are directly assigned** (or their assigned working group is).
3. **The activity is public (`public`)** within the team and has no exclusive assignees.
4. **They are a Team Manager** (Coordinator or Moderator): managers can inspect all team activities except strictly private activities of other users.

> [!NOTE]
> **Strict Privacy on Assigned Tasks**: If an activity has designated assignees and you are neither an assignee, creator, nor manager, the system protects operational privacy and conceals the activity from non-assigned members.

---

### 5.3. Who can MODIFY an activity or CHANGE ITS STATUS? (`update` & `changeStatus`)
Only the following users can modify properties and update lifecycle statuses (e.g., advancing from `draft` to `approved`, or `pending` to `completed`):
1. **The original Creator** of the activity.
2. **Any user directly Assigned** to the activity.
3. **Any member of a Working Group Assigned** to the activity.
4. **Team Coordinators & Moderators** (*Managers*).

If a member with read-only access attempts to change the status or edit fields, the system prevents the action, keeps the user on the current screen, and triggers a **friendly toast alert** informing them of missing modification privileges.

---

### 5.4. Authorization Matrix

| Action | Creator | Assignee / Group | Coordinator / Moderator | Read-only Member (Public) |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **View content** (`view`) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Add internal notes / comments** (`addNote`) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Attach supplementary files** (`attach`) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Modify title, description & fields** (`update`) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ *(Read-only)* |
| **Change operational status** (`changeStatus`) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ *(Read-only)* |
| **Reassign owners** (`assign`) | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Archive / Unarchive** (`archive`) | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Delete / Restore** (`delete`, `restore`) | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |

---

### 5.5. Team Roles & Configuration Locations

Permissions are configured across three intuitive layers in the application:

1. **Team Roles** (managed at `/teams/{team}/members`):
   - **Coordinator (`coordinator`)**: Full team administrator with master control over all activities, workflows, and memberships.
   - **Moderator (`moderator`)**: Operational manager (*Manager*). Can coordinate, edit, and update the status of team activities.
   - **Member (`user`)**: Standard operational user. Can only modify activities they created or were assigned to (individually or via their group). Holds read-only access to other public activities.
2. **Working Groups** (managed at `/teams/{team}/groups`):
   - Group colleagues by department or skill (e.g., *Legal*, *Design*, *DevOps*). Assigning an activity to a group instantly grants edit and status-change privileges to every member of that group.
3. **Activity Form** (during creation or editing):
   - **Visibility Selector**: Sets whether an item is `Public` (discoverable by the entire team) or `Private` (restricted to creator, assignees, and managers).
   - **Assignees Selector**: Selects which team members or groups have the mandate to execute and transition the activity.
