# ⚡ Activities & Workflow — SientiaMTX (v1.2.0)

In earlier versions of SientiaMTX, all work was modeled exclusively around "Tasks". As of **v1.2.0**, SientiaMTX has evolved into a **universal Activities (Items) architecture**.

An activity is not merely a to-do item: it is a polymorphic collaborative work object that faithfully mirrors how modern organizations operate (living documentation, formal decision-making, integrated meetings, multi-channel reminders, shared links, and operational tasks).

---

## 🧩 1. The 7 Activity Types

Each activity in SientiaMTX belongs to a specific subtype with its own dedicated UI, lifecycle, metadata schema, and tooling:

```
                  ┌── Task (task)
                  ├── Document (document)
                  ├── Note (note)
Activity (Item) ──┼── Link (link)
                  ├── Agreement / Decision (agreement)
                  ├── Meeting (meeting)
                  └── Reminder (reminder)
```

---

### 1.1. Task (`task`)
The core operational engine supercharged by the Eisenhower Matrix.
- **Lifecycle**: `Pending` ➔ `In Progress` ➔ `Completed` (or `Blocked`).
- **Prioritization**: Mandatory assignment of Urgency and Importance to populate the Eisenhower Matrix.
- **Subtasks**: Hierarchical nesting with dedicated subtask threads.
- **Timestamps**: Start date, deadline (`due_date`), and automated recording of the exact completion timestamp (`completed_at`).
- **Time Tracking**: Native integration with the real-time stopwatch and effort accounting system.

---

### 1.2. Document (`document`)
Collaborative document management with full in-browser office suite and graphics capabilities:
- **OnlyOffice Suite Integration**: Live simultaneous multi-user editing for `.docx`, `.xlsx`, and `.pptx` documents, hosted securely on your private infrastructure without third-party cloud leakage.
- **Filerobot Image Editor**: Advanced in-app image editor with cropping, rotation, color filters, annotations, and watermarks. Features dedicated `z-index` layer management to prevent modal collisions.
- **Chapters & Structuring**: Organize documents into structured text chapters with individual authoring and timestamps, or directly inject content generated by **Ax.ia**.
- **Version Control & Statuses**: `draft`, `review`, `approved`, `rejected`, and `archived`.

---

### 1.3. Note (`note`)
Central knowledge repository, quick briefings, and spontaneous ideas:
- **Rich Formatting**: Native support for clean Markdown and sanitized HTML.
- **Pinned Notes (`pinned`)**: Pin crucial reference information to the top of boards or dossiers.
- **AI Voice Memos**: Record audio directly from your microphone with instantaneous speech-to-text transcription powered by Ax.ia.

---

### 1.4. Link (`link`)
Intelligent bookmark and web reference library:
- **Automatic Open Graph Extraction**: When saving external URLs, SientiaMTX asynchronously fetches page metadata to extract title (`og:title`), description (`og:description`), and preview banner (`og:image`), rendering visually informative cards.
- **Broken Link Auditing**: Track resource health statuses (`active`, `broken`, `archived`) to keep team assets current.

---

### 1.5. Agreement & Decision (`agreement`)
Formal record of team consensus, board decisions, and binding guidelines:
- **Structured Fields**:
  - **Rationale (`rationale`)**: Contextual explanation of the business or technical reasoning behind the decision.
  - **Alternatives Considered**: Record of evaluated alternatives and trade-offs.
  - **Impact Level**: Impact classification (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`).
- **Decision Workflow**: States `Proposed` ➔ `Approved` ➔ `Rejected` / `Deferred` / `Superseded`.
- **Internal Member Digital Signature**: Tracking formal approval and digital consent from each assigned decision-maker.

---

### 1.6. Meeting (`meeting`)
Organization, scheduling, and tracking of in-person and digital sessions:
- **Flexible Modalities**:
  - **In-Person**: Physical address, room, or headquarters.
  - **Sientia Meet (Integrated Jitsi) 🎥**: Instant, secure video conference rooms with zero time limits and no external account sign-ups required.
  - **Smart Google Meet 🌐**: Direct sync with Google Calendar and smart link sanitation that auto-expands full URLs, shortlinks, or raw 10-character room codes (`abc-defg-hij`).
- **Agendas & Minutes**: Track planned topics (`agenda`), confirmed attendees, and recorded minutes (`minutes`).
- **Automated Closing**: Scheduled execution via `php artisan app:autocomplete-meetings` closes past meetings once scheduled time and duration expire with a grace period.

---

### 1.7. Reminder (`reminder`)
Time-sensitive notifications and call-to-actions across multiple channels:
- **Multi-Channel Dispatch**: Dispatch notifications via Email, Telegram Bot, WhatsApp, or Web Push notifications.
- **Smart Scheduling**: Configure alerts to fire $N$ minutes before due time (`notify_before_minutes`) or at an exact designated hour (`notify_at_hour`).
- **Live Countdown & Interactive Toggle Switch**: Renders a live countdown timer with an interactive toggle switch to mark reminders completed on demand, stopping the timer and canceling pending alert dispatches.
- **Background CLI Engine**: Automated batch dispatch handled via `php artisan reminders:trigger`.

SientiaMTX tailors visual displays to your operational workflow:

|---|---|---|---|

## ⏱️ 3. Effort Accounting & Time Tracking

To guarantee trustworthy workload tracking and comply with workplace labor regulations, SientiaMTX includes a full-featured **Time Tracking** suite:

```
[Start Timer] ────▶ [Global Sticky Header Banner] ────▶ [Stop Timer]
      │                                                      │
      ▼                                                      ▼
 Record TimeLog (task/activity)                         Calculate minutes & seconds
      │                                                      │
      └─────────────────▶ Effort Accounting Widget ◀─────────┘
```

### Core Tracking Features:
1. **Real-Time Stopwatch**: High-precision display (hours, minutes, seconds) directly inside activity headers and in the global top bar.
2. **Global Active Task Banner**: When a timer is running, a persistent top banner displays current progress, allowing users to pause, restart, or switch tasks seamlessly without losing context.
3. **Effort Accounting Dashboard Widget**:
   - Displays dedicated time breakdowns per user and activity.
   - **Deep Privacy Guaranteed**: If an activity is private, only authorized users can view its title and logged hours.
   - Automatically sorted by the user's most recent time log.
4. **Anomalous Workday Detection (`timelogs:mark-anomalous`)**:
   - Detects accidental forgotten stopwatches running beyond standard shifts or exceeding the **10-hour hard cap**.
   - Computes normalized `effectiveMinutes` to avoid skewing analytics and alerts coordinators for review.
